02 / Clinical Background

Not a blank face. Not your average therapist.

I’m a Specialist Psychologist with years of NHS and specialist experience working with every mental health issue you can imagine as well as complex trauma, PTSD/cPTSD, and people who have become absolute experts at intellectualising their feelings to avoid having to feel them.

When life gets intense, your brain adapts. What kept you safe as a kid or during chaos becomes an exhausting, automatic loop in adulthood. At Rewire Psychology, we don't just talk about the loop forever — we use actual evidence-based science (like EMDR and Interpersonal Psychotherapy) to actively disrupt and rewire it.

Clinical Candour You won't get a silent clinician clutching a clipboard. You get clinical precision. Warmth. Candour. And a very healthy dose of humour.
03 / Clinical Ethos

A Note on My Style (Please Read)

Therapy that stretches you — delivered with warmth and zero judgement.

If you’ve done talk therapy before, you probably know how to run rings around a passive therapist. You intellectualise, make a joke, tell a tidy story, and leave feeling exactly as stuck as when you walked in.

We don’t do that here.

I ask sharp questions. I’ll help you spot the exact moment your nervous system hits panic stations or runs away.

I won’t collude with your avoidance. If you’re hiding behind overthinking or perfectionism, I’ll call it out with compassion.

The Balance

Safety + challenge: Healing doesn’t happen by being coddled, nor does it happen by being lectured. It happens when you feel safe enough to be called on your habits.

If you show up, stay curious, and actually want to do the work — your internal wiring will shift.

Not by magic.

Not overnight.

But profoundly.
